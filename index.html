I work on multimodal foundation models, with a focus on reliability, grounded reasoning, and post-training. My recent research studies failure modes in vision-language models, particularly hallucination and visual grounding, and explores representation-level and inference-time interventions for improving their reliability.

I am also interested in reinforcement-learning-based post-training and how verifiable feedback can be used to improve multimodal reasoning and grounding.

Current research interests
  • Multimodal reasoning and visual grounding
  • VLM reliability, hallucination, and representation-level alignment
  • Reinforcement learning and post-training
  • Scalable training and inference

Research Philosophy

I am interested in understanding why multimodal models fail to remain grounded in their inputs, and how their internal representations change when this happens. My broader goal is to connect these insights with alignment and post-training methods that make multimodal reasoning more reliable without requiring expensive retraining.

Outside research, I enjoy reading, journaling research ideas, and watching sitcoms.

If you would like to collaborate, discuss research, or build something together, feel free to reach out by email.
