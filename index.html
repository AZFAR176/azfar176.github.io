Mohd Azfar

Research Engineer | Multimodal Foundation Models | LLM/VLM Post-Training | Trustworthy AI

I work on making large multimodal models more reliable in real systems. My focus is on post-training and alignment for LLMs/VLMs, preference optimization, and reducing hallucinations with stronger grounding and evaluation.

I enjoy building research that can move from paper ideas to deployable systems: better reward design, safer inference-time behavior, and practical agent routing.

GitHub Email
Current work emphasis: LLM/VLM post-training, reliability-first evaluation, safety-aware agent routing, and high-throughput vLLM inference.

Research goal: build multimodal systems that are reliably grounded, safer under distribution shift, and measurably less hallucinatory through strong representation-level alignment and inference-time control.

Outside research and engineering, I enjoy reading, watching sitcoms, and journaling ideas. I like translating abstract model behavior into practical intuition that teams can use.

On a lighter note, I spend a lot of time discussing ideas with AI. Some ideas are great, some are gloriously strange, and the best ones are always worth debating with humans.

If you would like to collaborate, discuss research, or build something together, feel free to reach out by email.
