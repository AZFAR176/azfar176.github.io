GitHub Email

I work on making large multimodal models more reliable in real systems. My research focuses on post-training and alignment for multimodal foundation models, with particular interests in preference optimization, representation learning, inference-time steering, and hallucination mitigation.

I enjoy studying how representation learning, reinforcement learning, and inference-time interventions can make foundation models more reliable while remaining practical for real-world deployment.

Current research explores
  • RLVR for foundation model post-training
  • Representation-level alignment
  • Inference-time steering
  • Hallucination mitigation
  • Agentic reasoning

Research goal: build multimodal systems that are reliably grounded, safer under distribution shift, and measurably less hallucinatory through strong representation-level alignment and inference-time control.

Research Philosophy

I am interested in understanding why multimodal foundation models fail and how representation learning, reinforcement learning, and inference-time interventions can make them more reliable. My long-term goal is to build foundation models whose reasoning and grounding can be systematically improved rather than simply scaled.

Outside research, I enjoy reading, journaling research ideas, and watching sitcoms.

If you would like to collaborate, discuss research, or build something together, feel free to reach out by email.
