Grounded Multimodal Reasoning Flow

1. Problem Reliable reasoning from smartphone clinical images under practical, noisy real-world conditions.
2. Grounding Clinical language to relevant visual regions so decisions depend on evidence, not priors.
3. Robustness Acquisition/viewpoint/nuisance variation and stability under shift.
4. Evidence Verification Controlled and counterfactual interventions to test whether predicted reasoning is causally supported.
5. Post-Training Grounding and counterfactual signals to verifiable rewards for RLVR optimization.
6. Future Direction Evidence-seeking multimodal agents: insufficient evidence -> acquire view/context -> re-ground -> decide.
