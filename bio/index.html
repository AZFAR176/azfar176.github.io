I work on reliable multimodal reasoning, with recent research on visual grounding, verifiable feedback, RL-based post-training, and agentic systems.

A central question in my work is how to verify that model predictions are supported by relevant visual evidence, and how this feedback can be used for post-training and active evidence acquisition.

Grounded Multimodal Reasoning & Evidence-Seeking Agents

My current direction builds from smartphone-based clinical vision toward models that can verify the evidence behind their predictions and, when that evidence is insufficient, actively seek additional information.

Research Flow

1. Grounding Use clinical descriptions to guide models toward task-relevant visual regions rather than relying primarily on learned priors.
↓
2. Robustness Test whether predictions and supporting regions remain stable under changes in acquisition, viewpoint, scale, illumination, and image quality.
↓
3. Counterfactual Verification Alter task-relevant visual evidence and test whether model predictions respond accordingly, providing a stronger test of evidence dependence.
↓
4. Verifiable Post-Training Convert grounding consistency and counterfactual sensitivity into verifiable reward signals for multimodal RLVR.
↓
5. Active Evidence Acquisition Extend passive verification toward agents that recognize insufficient evidence and request the observation or context needed before deciding.

Future Direction

Evidence-Seeking Multimodal Agents

A grounded model still has to make a decision from whatever evidence it receives. In real clinical settings, that evidence may be incomplete, ambiguous, or low quality.

I am interested in agents that can recognize this condition and select an informative next action - for example, requesting another view, examining a region more closely, asking for relevant clinical context, retrieving supporting information, or abstaining.

The broader question is whether multimodal agents can learn not only how to reason from evidence, but when and what additional evidence to seek.
