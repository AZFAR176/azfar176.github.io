4. Evidence-Seeking Multimodal Agent

Research Direction

What should the agent do when evidence is insufficient?

Formulation

\[s_t=(I_t,q,h_t,G_t,c_t)\]

\(I_t\): current observation, \(q\): clinical context/question, \(h_t\): interaction history, \(G_t\): grounding information, \(c_t\): evidence confidence.

\[ a_t \in \{\text{zoom},\ \text{new-view},\ \text{ask},\ \text{retrieve},\ \text{predict},\ \text{abstain}\} \]
\[ R=R_{\mathrm{task}}+\lambda_g R_{\mathrm{ground}}+\lambda_a R_{\mathrm{acq}}-\lambda_c C(a) \]

Agent loop

Image + Context ↓ Ground + Reason ↓ Evidence sufficient?
Yes → Predict / Abstain
No → Acquire missing evidence → New observation → Re-ground

\(R_{\mathrm{task}}\) rewards decision correctness, \(R_{\mathrm{ground}}\) rewards dependence on relevant visual evidence, \(R_{\mathrm{acq}}\) rewards informative acquisition, and \(C(a)\) discourages unnecessary actions.
